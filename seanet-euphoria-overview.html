04 · Running in the background · Warranty finder

Never pay for a repair that's still under warranty.

When a fault is submitted, Celeste searches Euphoria's own documents (the builder's delivery pack, manufacturer warranty cards, invoices) for cover on that equipment, and tells the crew before anyone orders the part.

For a 2025 Alpha build heading into its first yard period, that check runs on every fault, without anyone remembering to run it.

Background process · warranty finder04 / 09
05 · Running in the background · Documents and handover

One person does it once. Every crew member after them benefits.

Suggested documents

Celeste suggests which of your own documents belong to which equipment. One person accepts, and the document appears on every fault, work order, part and note for that equipment, from then on. Nothing is pulled from the web, and nothing is linked without approval.

Handover generation

Crew jot rough notes as they go. At crew change, Celeste compiles them into a handover grouped by department, every line linked to the exact equipment, fault or work order, then signed by outgoing, incoming and captain.

06 · Search and Show Related

Ask in plain words. Get Euphoria's own answer.

Search reaches inside every manual, scan, work order, fault, note and photo caption, not just file names. Open any result and Show Related surfaces everything else on board connected to it.

Every action the crew logs becomes searchable too, so the yacht is easier to find your way around on day 90 than on day one.
