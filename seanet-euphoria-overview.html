03 · What it costs each of you

Every rotation starts lower than the last.

Five crew, paper logbooks, no digital maintenance record. Each rotation inherits less than the one before.

  • Owners: you pay for maintenance you cannot see, and for repairs that may already have been covered.
  • Fleet captain: there is no record to show how well Euphoria is run.
  • Operations: another tool to evaluate is the last thing needed. This is one folder on one yacht.

04 · Not another AI chat

A note is not a record.

A line in a paper log, a WhatsApp message, an answer in a chat window: none has an author, a time or a source anyone can check later. Any answer for Euphoria has to be signed, sourced, kept through crew change and checkable by a third party, without extra work for the crew. A note meets none of these. A Celeste record meets all of them.

10 · The refit

The refit is where Euphoria loses the most.

Three to four months in Barcelona: many contractors, a stack of yard paperwork, Alpha warranty work mixed in with refit work, and the crew's attention on the yard. What is not recorded in that window is not recovered later, for the life of the yacht.
