05 · Onboarding

Point the file watcher at the root folder. Celeste does the rest.

No bulk upload, no spreadsheets to fill in, no migration project. One install on the computer that holds Euphoria's files, one folder chosen, and the crew carries on working.

The install, about fifteen minutes

01Register at registration.celeste7.ai and confirm with a one-time code.
02Download the installer for Mac or Windows, built for Euphoria.
03Choose the root folder. Network drives are detected automatically.
04Done. The watcher starts with the computer from then on.

The file watcher

Never edits your documents. It copies; nothing on the vessel is changed, moved or deleted.
Only the folder you choose, with boundary checks so nothing outside it is sent.
Fingerprints every file (SHA-256) and re-checks weekly, so a silent overwrite is caught.
Keeps going after onboarding. New and changed files flow in on their own; a changed file becomes a new version.
Handles poor connectivity. Offline, it pauses and resumes; a disconnected drive is never treated as deleted files.
Holds no database access. It trades a vessel credential, kept in the OS's secure store, for short-lived upload-only permits.

What happens to each file

Read

Text from PDFs, Office files and spreadsheets. Scans and photos through OCR.

Classify

What kind of document it is, and which department it belongs to.

Extract

Make, model, serial; certificate numbers and dates; work orders and intervals.

Check

Values checked against the source. Uncertain values are held back, not guessed.

De-duplicate

Merged only on a proving identifier: serial, part or certificate number.

Link

Manuals to equipment by maker and model; records to one another.

Publish

Into the live record, every record traceable to its source document.

A vessel of Euphoria's size (a few hundred to a few thousand files) is typically live within a day. For scale: on M/Y BC, roughly 8,500 files scattered across OneDrive with no PMS became roughly 10,500 interlinked, searchable records. Figures are approximate; every vessel's documents differ, and handwriting in older scans reads less reliably than print.

06 · Background process

Warranty finder

When a fault is submitted, Celeste searches Euphoria's own documents for warranty cover on that equipment (the builder's delivery pack, manufacturer warranty cards, invoices) and tells the crew whether a claim is possible, before a part is ordered.

Every finding links to the page it came from, so the crew and SeaNet can check the terms themselves. Euphoria is a 2025 build with Alpha warranty work already in play, going into a refit that will raise dozens of faults: the check runs on every one of them without anyone having to remember.

07 · Background process

Suggested documents: one person accepts, everyone finds it.

Celeste scans for documents that belong to a piece of equipment and suggests the link, with its source. One crew member accepts, and the document is attached to every fault, work order, part and note for that equipment, from then on.

Only your own documents. Nothing is pulled from the web.
Nothing links without approval. Declining leaves no trace; undo removes every link it created.
Survives crew change. The link belongs to the vessel, not the person who made it.
08 · Background process

Handover generation, from rough notes to a signed record.

Crew note things the way they'd say them. At crew change, Celeste compiles the notes and the logged work into a handover grouped by department, each line linked to the exact equipment, fault or work order it concerns.

The outgoing engineer signs, the incoming engineer acknowledges, the captain is on the chain, and the handover is sealed. The next person reads the yacht's actual state, not what someone remembered at 1 a.m. on their last night.

09 · Search and Show Related

Ask in plain words. Every result opens its source.

Search reaches inside every manual, scan, fault, work order, note, handover and photo caption, not just file names. Every action logged on Celeste is searchable too, so the record gets easier to use the more it's used. Show Related runs the search the other way: open any item and see everything connected to it.

10 · The ledger and the verifier

Nothing is overwritten. Anything sealed can be checked by anyone.

Append-only, attributed

Every action is recorded with who did it and when. A correction is a new entry that references the original; a deletion is struck through, never removed. Each event carries its own SHA-256 fingerprint.

Verifiable without an account

Sealed exports (a handover, a work order's history, a claim pack) can be sent to an owner, surveyor or insurer, who drops the file onto verifier.celeste7.ai. The check runs in their browser, uploads nothing, and confirms the record hasn't changed since it was sealed.

13 · Security

Seven independent layers between the internet and a vessel's records.

Microsoft 365 sign-in with a second device; Celeste never sees the password.
Approved crew list per vessel. A valid Microsoft login that isn't on it is refused.
Signed access pass on every request; any alteration invalidates it.
Vessel and role resolved by the server, never taken from the user's device.
Every query scoped to the vessel; cross-vessel references are rejected.
Row-level rules on every table in the database itself.
Separate identity and records databases; access to one gives nothing in the other.
Encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256), daily backups, never used to train AI.
Incidents reported within 72 hours of confirmation.
At contract end, original files and records returned, then deleted within 30 days.

15 · What's not included

Focused on engineering, on purpose.

Celeste does the engineering record properly rather than everything partly. These stay in the tools SeaNet already uses:

Hours of rest: dedicated MLC tools already do this well and cheaply.
Crew contracts, payroll and accommodation: crew HR, not engineering.
Charter operations: stay on SeaNet's own platform.
Purchasing and receiving: parts and inventory are tracked; procurement stays with your existing process.
Class system of record: Celeste is the operational layer; it does not replace a class-approved system.
