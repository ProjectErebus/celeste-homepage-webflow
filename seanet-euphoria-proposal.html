04 · Onboarding

Legacy systems take weeks to go live. Euphoria is live in one day.

No bulk upload, no spreadsheets, no migration project. One install, one folder chosen, and the crew carries on working. Proven on M/Y BC: no PMS, years of files across OneDrive, every one read and linked.

Copies, never edits. It never changes, moves or deletes a document.
Only the chosen folder. Nothing outside it is sent.
Fingerprints every file (SHA-256), so a silent overwrite is caught.
Works offline. It pauses and resumes; no database access.

Each file is read (scans through OCR), classified, checked against its source and linked. Records merge only on a serial, part or certificate number. Uncertain values are held back, not guessed.

05 · Over time

Every other system decays. This one compounds.

Every fault, note and link adds to the record. The longer Euphoria runs on Celeste, the more it knows.

06 · Warranty

Euphoria pays for repairs the builder owes. Celeste stops it.

Every fault is checked against Euphoria's own warranty documents (delivery pack, warranty cards, invoices) before a part is ordered. Every finding links to its source.

07 · Handover

Today a handover is a few rough pages. The rest leaves on the crew flight.

At crew change, Celeste compiles the notes and logged work into a handover, every line linked to its record. Outgoing signs, incoming acknowledges, the captain is on the chain.

08 · Documents

Every new person hunts for the same manual. Link it once. Never hunt it again.

Celeste suggests where a document belongs, with its source. One crew member accepts, and it is on every record that needs it, through every crew change.

09 · Search

The answer is aboard. Nobody can find it.

Today: binders, inboxes and a call to the last engineer, while the job waits. On Celeste: one search that reads inside every manual, scan, note and photo caption.

10 · Proof · a world first

On paper, nobody can prove what was done. On Celeste, anyone can check.

Every entry keeps its source, time and person, and is sealed. The first public verifier for yacht records shows whether a sealed file was changed. No account, nothing uploaded.

11 · ISM package · $3,495 per vessel per month

Every port, the same evidence pack is rebuilt. On Celeste it is saved once.

The ISM package adds the safety-management record to the same system, signed, sealed and linked to the equipment and crew it concerns.

Non-conformities, logged as they happen.
Corrective actions, closed with evidence.
Drills, with attendance and outcome.
Statutory inspections, flagged before they lapse.
Safety equipment, with service history and expiry.
SMS documents, controlled and versioned.

13 · Security

Only Euphoria's crew see Euphoria's records. Nobody else.

Microsoft 365 sign-in, second device as set in SeaNet's policy. Celeste never sees the password.
Approved crew list per vessel. Any other login is refused.
Vessel and role set by the server, checked again in the database on every table.
Encrypted in transit and at rest, backed up daily.
No AI training on vessel data.
At contract end, files and records returned, then deleted within 30 days.

15 · What's not included

Focused on engineering, on purpose.

Hours of rest: dedicated MLC tools already do this well.
Crew contracts, payroll, accommodation: crew HR, not engineering.
Charter operations: stay on SeaNet's own platform.
Purchasing and receiving: parts are tracked; procurement stays as it is.
Class system of record: Celeste is the operational layer alongside it.
