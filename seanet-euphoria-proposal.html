02 · What the current setup costs

Five leaks, each already costing Euphoria.

  • The galley appliance. No fault trail, so any warranty claim rests on someone's memory, and a claim that cannot be evidenced can be refused.
  • The customs boarding. A crew list that did not match who was aboard, at the moment it was asked for.
  • Crew rotation. At 50%+ annual turnover in some superyacht roles, what one crew learns about Euphoria leaves with them.†
  • Resending. The same manual, invoice or photo hunted for by every new person, every time.
  • Paper logbooks. Nothing an owner, surveyor, insurer or flag inspector can check. ISM deficiencies are the leading cause of port state control detentions.‡

06 · A note is not a record

The model is one part. The record around it is the product.

An answer in a chat window has no author, no time and no source, and it is gone when the window closes. Celeste keeps records: attributed, timestamped, linked to their source, approved by a named crew member, and kept through every crew change.

The file watcher

  • Never edits your documents. It copies; nothing on the vessel is changed, moved or deleted.
  • Only the folder you choose, with boundary checks so nothing outside it is sent.
  • Fingerprints every file (SHA-256) and re-checks weekly, so a silent overwrite is caught.
  • Continues after onboarding. New and changed files follow on their own; a changed file becomes a new version.
  • Tolerates poor connectivity. Offline, it pauses and resumes; a disconnected drive is never read as deleted files.
  • Holds no database access. A vessel credential in the operating system's secure store is exchanged for short-lived, upload-only permits.

What happens to each file

ReadText from PDFs, Office files and spreadsheets; scans and photos through OCR
ClassifyThe kind of document, and the department it belongs to
ExtractMake, model, serial; certificate numbers and dates; work orders and intervals
CheckValues checked against the source; uncertain values are held back, not guessed
De-duplicateRecords merged only on a proving identifier: serial, part or certificate number
LinkManuals to equipment by maker and model; records to one another
PublishInto the live record, every record traceable to its source document

A yacht of Euphoria's size (a few hundred to a few thousand files) is typically live within a day. For scale: M/Y BC's roughly 8,500 scattered OneDrive files, with no PMS, became roughly 10,500 linked, searchable records. Figures approximate; handwriting in older scans reads less reliably than print.

09 · Background process · warranty

Warranty money is lost to missing paperwork, not bad luck.

Every fault raised without the warranty checked is a repair that may be paid for twice. With Celeste, when a fault is entered, Celeste searches Euphoria's own documents (the builder's delivery pack, manufacturer warranty cards, invoices) for cover on that equipment, and shows the clause before a part is ordered.

Euphoria is a 2025 build with Alpha warranty work already open, going into a refit that will raise dozens of faults. The check runs on every one of them; each finding links to the page it came from.

10 · Background process · linked documents

Every new person hunts for the same files.

Today the manual is found by asking whoever last used it, or it is resent. With Celeste, one person links a document and everyone finds it. Celeste suggests which of Euphoria's own documents belong to a piece of equipment, with the source. One crew member accepts, and the document is attached to every fault, work order and part for that equipment from then on.

  • Only Euphoria's documents. Nothing is pulled from the web.
  • Nothing links without approval. A declined suggestion leaves no trace; undo removes every link it created.
  • The link belongs to the vessel, not to the person who made it, so it survives crew change.

11 · Background process · handover

Every crew change, someone starts from zero.

Today the handover is whatever the leaving engineer remembers to write on the last night. With Celeste it already exists. Crew note things as they would say them. At crew change, Celeste compiles the notes and the closed work into a handover grouped by department, each line linked to the equipment, fault or work order it concerns. The outgoing engineer signs, the incoming engineer acknowledges, the captain is on the chain, and the handover is sealed.

13 · The ledger and the verifier

On paper, nobody can prove what was done.

An insurer, a surveyor, an owner or a flag inspector can ask, and a paper log cannot answer. With Celeste nothing is overwritten and anything sealed can be checked by anyone. Every action is recorded with who did it and when. A correction is a new entry that references the original; a deletion is struck through, never removed. Each event carries its own SHA-256 fingerprint.

15 · Crew, fleet and vessel isolation

Each crew sees their yacht. Management sees the fleet.

  • Crew page. Crew record who is aboard, with times on board and away, so the list is current when a boarding party arrives.
  • Fleet view. SeaNet's managers move between vessels on one login and see what needs a decision across all of them, without opening each record.
  • Isolation. Each vessel's records are scoped on every request and in the database itself. The crew of one yacht never see another's.
16 · Security

Seven independent layers between the internet and a vessel's records.

  • Microsoft 365 sign-in with a second device; Celeste never sees the password.
  • Approved crew list per vessel. A valid login that is not on it is refused.
  • Signed access pass on every request; any alteration invalidates it.
  • Vessel and role resolved by the server, never taken from the user's device.
  • Every query scoped to the vessel; cross-vessel references are rejected.
  • Row-level rules on every table in the database itself.
  • Separate identity and records databases; access to one gives nothing in the other.
  • Encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256), with daily backups.
  • Incidents reported within 72 hours of confirmation.
  • At contract end, original files and records returned, then deleted within 30 days.

18 · What is not included

Limited to engineering, on purpose.

  • Hours of rest. Dedicated tools already do this.
  • Crew contracts, payroll and accommodation. Crew HR, not engineering.
  • Charter operations. Remain on SeaNet's own platform.
  • Purchasing and receiving. Parts and inventory are tracked; procurement stays with SeaNet's existing process.
