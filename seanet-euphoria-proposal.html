04 · Onboarding

Legacy systems take weeks to go live. Euphoria is live in about a day.

No bulk upload, no spreadsheets, no migration project. One install, one folder chosen, and the crew carries on working. Proven on M/Y BC: no PMS, years of files across OneDrive, every one read and linked.

The install

01Register at registration.celeste7.ai.
02Install on the computer that holds Euphoria's files. Choose the root folder.
03Done. New and changed files keep flowing in on their own.

The file watcher

Copies, never edits. It never changes, moves or deletes a document.
Only the chosen folder. Nothing outside it is sent.
Fingerprints every file (SHA-256), so a silent overwrite is caught.
Works offline. It pauses and resumes; no database access.

Each file is read (scans through OCR), classified, checked against its source and linked. Records merge only on a serial, part or certificate number. Uncertain values are held back, not guessed. Handwriting reads less reliably than print.

05 · Warranty

The builder may already owe Euphoria this repair.

Every fault is checked against Euphoria's own warranty documents (delivery pack, warranty cards, invoices) before a part is ordered. Every finding links to its source.

A recent Alpha build going into a refit will raise many faults. The check runs on each, without anyone having to remember.

06 · Handover

Today a handover is a few rough pages. The rest leaves on the crew flight.

Crew note things the way they'd say them. At crew change, Celeste compiles the notes and logged work into a handover, every line linked to its record.

Outgoing signs, incoming acknowledges, the captain is on the chain, and it is sealed.

07 · Search

The answer is aboard. Nobody can find it.

Today: binders, inboxes and a call to the last engineer, while the job waits. On Celeste: one search that reads inside every manual, scan, note and photo caption, and shows everything connected.

08 · Proof · a world first

On paper, nobody can prove what was done. On Celeste, anyone can check.

Every action is recorded with who and when, and sealed. A correction is a new entry; nothing is erased. The first public verifier for yacht records lets an owner, surveyor or insurer drop in a sealed file and see whether it changed. It runs in their browser and uploads nothing.

11 · Security

Euphoria's records are seen only by the people who should see them.

Approved crew list per vessel. Any other login is refused.
Vessel and role set by the server, checked again in the database on every table.
Encrypted in transit and at rest, backed up daily.

13 · What's not included

Focused on engineering, on purpose.

Hours of rest: dedicated MLC tools already do this well.
Crew contracts, payroll, accommodation: crew HR, not engineering.
Charter operations: stay on SeaNet's own platform.
Purchasing and receiving: parts are tracked; procurement stays as it is.
Class system of record: Celeste is the operational layer alongside it.
