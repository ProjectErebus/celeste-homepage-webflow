2. Cookies and Similar Technologies

2.1 Cookies. Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device. "First-party" cookies are set by the site you are visiting. "Third-party" cookies are set by another company, such as an analytics provider, and can be used to recognize your browser across different websites.

2.2 Similar technologies. These include:

  • local storage, which lets a website keep information in your browser;
  • service-worker caches, which let a web app store copies of pages and data so it can work offline; and
  • tags (small pieces of code), which send information about your visit to a provider.

In this notice, "cookies" includes these similar technologies unless we say otherwise.

The Verifier uses no cookies, no browser storage and no analytics, and loads no files from third parties: its fonts are served from the Verifier site itself. It runs entirely in your browser, and the file you check is never uploaded.

7. Your Choices

7.1 Consent banner and settings. Accept, reject or adjust analytics, advertising and session-analytics technologies at any time at the "Your Privacy Choices" link in the footer of every page. Rejecting them does not affect your use of the Websites.

7.2 Global Privacy Control. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control ("GPC") signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information and of targeted advertising. We will not enable advertising technologies for that browser. If you had previously accepted them, GPC overrides that choice for as long as your browser sends the signal.

7.3 "Your Privacy Choices". US residents can also opt out at the "Your Privacy Choices" link in the footer of our Websites or by emailing contact@celeste7.ai.

7.4 Google and Meta controls. You can manage Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com. You can also block Google Analytics with Google's opt-out browser add-on (tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). For Meta, you can manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram Accounts Center.

7.5 Browser controls. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies and clear site data. Clearing site data for app.celeste7.ai signs you out of the App and removes its offline cache. Signing out of the App also removes the offline cache. Clearing site data for collective.celeste7.ai signs you out of the Collective Site.

7.6 Do Not Track. There is no common standard for "Do Not Track" browser signals, so we do not respond to them. We do honor GPC (Section 7.2).

7.7 Scope of choices. Choices made through the banner, GPC or browser settings apply only to the browser and device on which you make them.
